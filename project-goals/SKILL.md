# Project Goals

Turn an initial idea into a concise product brief. Use `grill-me` when discovery needs a deeper collaborative interview; otherwise ask only questions that would change scope, success, users, or delivery constraints.

Separate the user problem from the proposed solution. Identify the primary user and job to be done, the desired change in behavior or business outcome, and evidence of value. Make non-goals explicit to prevent accidental expansion.

Produce: goal; users and critical use cases; observable success criteria; must-have scope, deferred work, and non-goals; constraints and risks; and open decisions with a recommended next design step. Do not manufacture metrics or requirements, choose technologies, design a database, or build UI.
